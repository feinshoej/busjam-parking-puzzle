Bus Jam: Parking Puzzle

A colourful parking-lot puzzle for iPhone: tap buses out of a jammed lot so everyone waiting at the bus stop gets a seat.

How to play

  • Tap a bus to drive it out of the lot the way it faces. If another bus is in the way, it bumps and stays put.
  • A bus that gets out drives round to one of the 5 bays at the bus stop.
  • The person at the front of the line boards a parked bus of their colour. Full buses leave and free their bay.
  • Clear the level by getting everyone a seat. If every bay is full and nobody at the front can board, you're out of space.
  • Grey buses with a ? show their colour once their way out is clear.
  • Levels come in stages of 5, each ending with a hard level. Run out of space and you go back to the start of your stage; clear the hard level to reach the next checkpoint.
  • Each colour also has its own symbol, so you can play without telling colours apart.
