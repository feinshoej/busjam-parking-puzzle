Bus Jam: Parking Puzzle

A colourful parking-lot puzzle for iPhone: tap buses out of a jammed lot so everyone waiting at the bus stop gets a seat.

How to play

  • Tap a bus to drive it out of the lot. The arrow on its roof shows which way it goes. If another bus is in the way, it bumps and stays put.
  • A bus that gets out drives round to one of the 5 bays at the bus stop.
  • Passengers walk round a loop at the stop. Anyone in the loop boards a parked bus of their colour; the others wait in line to join. Full buses leave and free their bay.
  • Clear the level by getting everyone a seat. If every bay is full and nobody in the loop can board, you're out of space and can try the level again.
  • Grey buses with a ? show their colour once their way out is clear. Every 5th level is a hard level.
  • Turn on Colour Symbols in Settings to show each colour's symbol, if colours are hard to tell apart.
